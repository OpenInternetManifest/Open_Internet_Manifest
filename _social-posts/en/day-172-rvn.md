### RVN: The Hague court – the plug is in dollars

The US is preparing sanctions against the International Criminal Court as a whole, not only against judges.  
The Netherlands has been warned. Pay, witnesses, the cells: The Hague is looking at how the court keeps running if the dollar is locked.

Rubio: dismantle it brick by brick.  
The trigger: the warrant for Netanyahu, and earlier a probe into US forces in Afghanistan. The US never joined.

Two frames.  
One: a court without a mandate over non-members hunts allies. Sovereignty.  
The other: whoever controls the dollar dismantles the law when the verdict does not suit.

Both touch a piece.  
Neither is the text of the sanction. That text is not out yet.

### What stands

Judges and prosecutors are already on lists: mail, bank, travel.  
Now the institution. A six-to-seven-month grace period, the WSJ writes. After that, no services from US firms without an OFAC licence.

125 member states. Nine hundred people. Seat: The Hague.  
The host may keep the building open. The host does not run SWIFT.

### The pattern

First the verdict.  
Then sovereignty.  
Then the till.

Same plug as the badge at the White House and the broadcaster in Hilversum.  
Whoever closes the payments need not respect the ruling.  
Whoever only says “international law” pretends law exists without a bank.

### OIM way out

Do not canonise the court.  
Do not treat every American sanction as liberation.

Do: is the measure on paper.  
What still works in euros.  
What Washington asks of the Netherlands as host — withdraw, or only the bill.

Law that lives only as long as Silicon Valley forwards the mail is the shop window.  
The till is the dollar system.

**Question for you**

When the verdict does not suit…  
should the court give way, or the bank?
