### RVN: Ilhan Omar – the name that came back

In 2013 Ilhan Omar posted on Instagram:  
“Happy Father’s Day to my aabo Nur Said…”  

In 2019 she deleted that post.  
In 2026 a court filing refers to her as “Ilhan Nur Said Elmi, a/k/a Ilhan Abdullahi Omar”.

The post is back. The filing exists. The claim is old.

Omar was married to Ahmed Nur Said Elmi from 2009 to 2017. Critics say he is her brother. Omar denies it. There is no DNA evidence or court ruling. In a recent interview she said of a possible family relation: “Not to my knowledge.”

Her office maintains: “Ilhan Omar is her real name.”  
The filing uses the other name.  
The deleted post calls her father “Nur Said”.

### The pattern

First the rumor.  
Then silence.  
Then a post that disappears.  
Then silence.  
Then a home search.  
Then a filing.  
Then the old post again.

The same rhythm. Information that comes, disappears, and returns with a piece of paper.

### What this means

A filing is not a conviction.  
A deleted post is not proof.  
A denial is not an acquittal.

What does stand: there is an ongoing federal investigation, there was a home search, and there is now a name in a court filing that previously lived only in rumors and one deleted post.

### OIM way out

Don’t adopt the claim.  
Don’t treat the denial as the end.

Do: what is in the filing.  
What was in the post.  
What has been investigated.  
What has not been investigated.

Verifiable records above a story that comes and goes.

**Question for you**

How often have you seen a story disappear…  
only to return later with a piece of paper?
