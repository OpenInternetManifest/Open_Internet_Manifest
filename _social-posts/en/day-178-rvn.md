### RVN: Trump and Xi – a lot of carpet, little deal

Last week China’s president Xi was with Trump in Washington.  
A greeting at the airport. Dinner. Tea. A look at the US Constitution together.

The United Nations was meeting that same week in New York. Xi did not go. In 2015 he did both.

On television it sounded big.  
Trump: the farmers will be happy.  
Xi: calm between the two great powers.

What was actually agreed is smaller.

### What is on the table

The United States and China had a temporary pause in their trade war. That pause was due to end in November.  
It has now been extended to 10 January. Two extra months. After that they meet again: November in China, December in Miami.

They published lists of goods on which import tariffs may come down.  
From China to America: toys, Christmas baubles, small appliances.  
From America to China: corn, milk, medical equipment.  
Soybeans — important for US farmers — were not on China’s list.  
When the tariff actually falls is not firmly dated.

Also: they will talk about artificial intelligence.  
America says China will buy US coal. That was not always in the Chinese text.

Taiwan — the island China treats as its own — did appear in the Chinese readout. Xi wants America to be “prudent”. Taiwan was not in the American press release.

### How it works

First the party.  
Then “we agree”.  
Then the small print.

Anyone who only sees the photo thinks the quarrel is over.  
Anyone who sees the date sees delay.

Same trick as Budget Day.  
A fine sentence on the board.  
The real number in the annex.

### OIM way out

Do not turn the handshake into treason.  
Do not turn the handshake into peace.

Do look at how long the pause runs.  
Which products. Which tariff. Which date.  
What China says about Taiwan and America leaves out.

A summit without a text is the shop window.  
Two extra months is the till.

**Question for you**

If they will see each other in November anyway…  
was this a deal, or buying time until January?
