### RVN: X open source – shadowbanning becomes a lot harder

Elon Musk has announced that the **full codebase** of X is going open source.

Not just the algorithm.  
Everything. Every line of code that touches the system.

Third parties will be allowed to verify that the published code is actually running in production.  
No exceptions.

### What this means

Shadowbanning, silent visibility filtering, secret downranking…  
Those kinds of actions become a lot harder.

Anyone with technical knowledge will soon be able to see for themselves how the machine works.  
No more “just trust us.”  
Only: here is the code, check it yourself.

Elon ties it explicitly to one sentence:  
“Only total transparency deserves trust.”

### The broader context

He has previously called out European leaders openly on secret censorship deals.  
Platforms that quietly cooperated versus X that refused.

This is the logical next step:  
not just claiming to be transparent, but making it enforceable.
