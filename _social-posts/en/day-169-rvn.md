### RVN: Greenland – permanent control, or a signature

Friday night: a deal.  
United States, Denmark, Greenland. Text not yet public. Signing next week in New York.

Trump: permanent control over security and “all other needs”. No cost. No adversary with a base or sensitive investment without written US approval. A larger military presence on top.

Copenhagen and Nuuk: it strengthens Arctic security. And it recognises sovereignty and the Greenlanders’ right of self-determination.

Same paper. Two shop windows.

### What is fixed, what is not

Fixed: Pituffik/Thule is already there. China and Russia in the Arctic are not fan fiction. Trump earlier threatened to take the island.

Not fixed: what “permanent control” is in law. What “all other needs” covers. Who rejects “sensitive investments”. Whether Greenland’s parliament reads this the way Truth Social does.

Reuters: it is not a purchase. It is more basing, less room for non-NATO.  
Until the text is online, the rest is translation.

### The pattern

First the threat.  
Then the agreement.  
Then two victory sentences.

Anyone who only reads Trump hears an island.  
Anyone who only reads Frederiksen hears NATO calm.  
Anyone who misses the pdf picks a camp.

Same reflex as the 9/11 storm post: the headline harder than the file.

### OIM way out

Not “Trump steals Greenland”.  
Not “nothing happened”.

Do: where the text is.  
Which articles on bases, investment, duration.  
What Nuuk itself signs, not only Denmark.

Sovereignty that exists only in a press release is the shop window.  
A treaty you can read is the till.

or is the Truth Social line already enough?
