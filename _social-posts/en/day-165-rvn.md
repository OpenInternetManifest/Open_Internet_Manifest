### RVN: LG – you bought the screen, they claim the room

Gamers Nexus and Level1Techs put an LG smart TV on the meter. Packet captures, not a column.

What stands firm: the TV scans the home network. Phones, watches, printers. Devices that never talk to the screen. Plus nearby Wi-Fi, signal strength, location. What you watch, including via HDMI and other inputs. That goes, among other places, to LG Ad Solutions.

LG does not entirely deny that. A network scan is, they say, a smart-TV feature. ACR – recognising what you watch – is standard in the industry.

The microphone part is the dispute.

Researchers captured microphone audio while the screen appeared off, including offline. File stored locally. Upload once a network is back. Voice commands as plain text in logs. After a wake word, residual audio hung on for seconds.

LG says: no ambient conversations. Only if you press the voice button or have “Hi LG” switched on. Wake word local, then gone.

That is the press release.
