### RVN: France – the town hall is burning

17 October. Around 550,000 on the streets according to the Interior Ministry. More than 1,200 arrests. Sixty officers injured.

Limoges: the town hall on fire. Marseille: town hall broken into. Lyon: tourist office on fire. Paris: tear gas, fire, looting into the evening. Rouen: 9,000 started, calm. Then Swarovski, Lacoste, Sephora, Micromania, Fnac looted. Fire in the street.

People saw their shop emptied. A life's work in one evening.

### Who was walking, and who came back

Not the schoolkid with the banner. Groups in black, masks. In Paris French and Palestinian flags on the same square.

In Rouen the Marche des Normands. First banned, then allowed. They clashed with the counter-demonstrators. The police were there. They also pulled back. Citizens who call themselves patriots stood on the street the state temporarily left.

Macron wrote on X: the sowers of chaos will be prosecuted. Lecornu pointed to the ultra-left. The images were already there. The police line gave way.

### The split

The story is “youth protest against the cost of living”.  
The reality is a town hall burning, shops being emptied, and people holding the street themselves because the police do not.

Whether the politics wants the chaos is the second question. What stands: the chaos comes out. The state gives way. The citizen who does not want his city burned steps in himself. That is not a plan. That is the outcome.

### OIM way out

Do not turn every march into a civil war.  
Do not leave “schoolkids” standing when the historic town hall is on fire.

Do: who is walking, what is being destroyed, and whether the state stays or gives way.  
A state that leaves the street to whoever throws hardest forces the other to regulate himself. That is not order. That is a gap.

**Question for you**

If the police give way and the patriot takes the street…  
who still holds the city?
