### RVN: Albert Heijn – part 2, the shelves

Yesterday: outage or attack. Two doors.

Today: the shelves. Fresh is gone. Distribution centres were at a standstill, some are running again. Ahold: no indication that customer data was stolen. Site, app and delivery still down. Investigation ongoing. No culprit, no ransom note.

At the same time, a different method. France: hundreds of supermarkets, cooling taken over, 50 degrees, food gone. Software partly without updates. NCSC: no link with Ahold.

And kinetic. Ukraine hit three Yandex data centres. Taxis and banking dropped out. The hall itself wrecked.

Three blows. Network off. Thermostat taken over. Server hall hit. Same lesson: food and data hang on systems you can switch off.

Tomorrow the full count, if more is on the table.

**Question for you**

Empty shelves after one Sunday afternoon…  
was the attack large, or the backup thin?
