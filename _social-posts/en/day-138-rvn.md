### RVN: Why are the anti-vaxxers furious… and the vaccinated silent?

Last week the first texts from Anthony Fauci’s government iPhone were released.

On 25 January 2021 he privately wrote to Walensky and Murthy that a cytokine storm and fever after the second dose “theoretically could be associated with miscarriage in the 1st trimester.”

Nine days later he publicly stated:  
“no red flags about that, about pregnant women.”

Privately: a warning.  
Publicly: absolute reassurance.

**And then something remarkable happened.**

The people who refused the shot from the start reacted with fury.  
They saw their skepticism confirmed: different things were said behind closed doors than in front of the cameras.

The people who took the shot… remained largely silent.

No mass outrage.  
No demands for clarification.  
No “we were lied to.”

**Why?**

Because admitting you were misled means admitting you made a wrong judgment yourself.  
That you placed trust in institutions that did not deserve it.  
That social pressure, fear, and the “follow the science” mantra were stronger than your own critical thinking.

Cognitive dissonance is a powerful mechanism.  
The greater the investment (the shot, social status, moral superiority), the harder it becomes to accept the new facts.

The silence of the vaccinated is not proof that nothing is wrong.  
It is proof that it hurts to acknowledge that something *was* wrong.

**OIM way out**

Informed consent only exists when people receive the real information – including the doubts that were discussed behind closed doors.

Keep reading the primary sources.  
Keep asking questions.  
Even when it is uncomfortable for those who already said “yes.”

**Question for you**

If the private messages show that risks were seen which were publicly denied…  
why is the greatest silence found exactly where the greatest investment sits?
