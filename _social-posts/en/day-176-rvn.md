### RVN: The third rail in rock

Billy Corgan (@BillyCorgan), Smashing Pumpkins, this week on his own podcast.  
I am a Christian. Jesus as Lord. If it costs the career, so be it.
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rra8irnCl1w

He quoted himself to Rolling Stone: God is the third rail in rock.  
Touch that line and you throw your career down the hole.  
Now he puts his hand on it. No fog. No “spiritual, sort of”.

Alice Cooper did it years ago. Shock rock, guillotine, snakes — and then the same confession, in the open, for years. Not a children’s choir. The same world.  
He is the precedent. Corgan is the bang: the man from Mellon Collie, from the scene that wore 666 on the shirt as if that were courage.

### What it exposes

In pop, trauma is allowed. Drugs. Politics. Identity. Occult aesthetics.  
Christ makes the room go quiet — or venomous.

Not because nobody may believe in God.  
Because the microphone belongs to the industry.  
YouTube is the file: the comments are rarely about the record. They are about whether he still belongs to the club.

Corgan said it himself: if I cannot be honest about who I am, what am I asking a fan to believe in?

### The pattern

First the shirt.  
Then the hymn in the fog — Zwan, a line, a wink.  
Then the sentence without fog.

The taboo is not a law.  
It is a counter.  
Anyone who names the Name tests whether the gate still belongs to the scene.

Same cut as faith and religion.  
The conscience is his.  
The label, the playlist, the comment section want to rent the conscience.

### OIM way out

Do not turn every conversion into a trend.  
Do not canonise the record.

Do: which god may appear on the record.  
Do: faith is not membership of the label.

Hashes for texts. Not for souls.  
The microphone is a gate as well.  
Corgan opened it for a moment.

**Question for you**

Which confession is allowed on your timeline…  
and which is immediately “career over”?
