### RVN: Malieveld – the label was there before the first stone

Saturday, Malieveld. Wij Zijn Het Volk.  
Headlines: far right. Hitler salute. 24 arrests. Jetten: repulsive.

Part of that happened.  
Fireworks, a pole, riot police, an emergency order. Anyone who throws is no longer a demonstrator.

Part of it was there before the first stone.

### What came first

Half past eleven: calm. Not a war.  
Still: black as the dress code. Faces covered. That projects something. That is not innocent fashion.

From the stage: no march if the coverings stay. Arrests.  
Oil on the fire.  
Because the same covering has been tolerated at other protests for years — and in the covid years was briefly mandatory.

The march was barely allowed to start. Kettled. A long wait. Three metres, not five hundred.  
Anyone who wanted to leave after it escalated was not allowed out at first. Advice: go towards the rioting group. That is not an exit. That is a kettle.

### Two frames

One: hooligans looking for a clash. Prince’s flag, salute, remigration scarf. Done.

The other: police who ban coverings and then play the squad game.

Both can hold a piece.  
Undercovers in masks who egg people on: possible. Not proven. Anyone who drops that as fact does the same as the headline.

XR on the A12 the same day: Jetten “unacceptable”. Two sentences, two weights. Measure the rule, not only the flag.

### The pattern

First the label.  
Then the atmosphere.  
Then the stone.  
Then the label was right.

Face covering is the hinge.  
At one demo a right. At the other a reason to dissolve.  
Same mouth. Different shirt.

### OIM way out

Do not excuse the salute.  
Do not turn the riot police into a plot.

Do split.

Who stood there at 11.30 without a mask.  
Who threw.  
Who was not allowed to leave.  
Which rule applied last year on the Malieveld, and at XR.

Film. Hash. The wording of the orders.  
The stamp is not a police report.

**Question for you**

Did you see a demo that derailed…  
or a label that had already drawn the route?
