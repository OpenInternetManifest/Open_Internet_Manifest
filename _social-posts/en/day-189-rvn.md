### RVN: If I were the devil – 1965

Paul Harvey, ABC Radio, 3 April 1965. An essay he had already put in the newspaper in 1964. “If I were the devil.” https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jnPE8u5ONls

If I were the devil, I would subvert the churches first. Whisper: do as you please. To the young: the Bible is a myth. What’s bad is good. What’s good is square. Teach the old to pray to Washington. Then the rest. Lurid literature that makes everything else dull. Dirtier films. Drugs. Alcohol. Pills. Families at war with themselves. Churches. Nations. Until each in its turn is consumed.

He repeated it in later years. The core stayed: remove the standard, set the family against itself, give the media the flame, let the state take the place of the father.

### What stands

No revelation. A radio man who in 1965 wrote down how you empty a society from the inside. Start with the church. Whisper that the book is a myth. Make good and evil reversible. Let the families fight themselves. Give the media the flame. Teach people to pray to the government.

Sixty years later the clip circulates because the lines are recognisable. Not because Harvey saw the future. Because the steps he named were taken. Not everywhere. Not as a plan. As a pattern.

### The split

The story is “prophecy”.  
The reality is a checklist. Make the Bible a myth. Make the family a battlefield. Make the media the accelerant. Make the state the father.

That is not a devil with a beard. It is a method. Whoever removes the standard, loosens the family and lets the centre be the only source of truth does not need a plan. The outcome is the same.

### OIM way out

Do not canonise Harvey.  
Do not turn every cultural shift into Satan.

Do: lay the checklist next to the outcome.  
A society that loses the standard, lets the family fight and gives the media the flame has no second leg. It has the shop window.

Whoever has the node at least has the count. The rest is whispering.
