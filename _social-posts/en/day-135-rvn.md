### RVN: Fauci & the Tie – Josh Hawley’s Trap

**Short recap**

Earlier this week, Anthony Fauci appeared before Senator Rand Paul’s Homeland Security Committee.  
He invoked the Fifth Amendment 111 times.  
Questions about the origin of the virus, gain-of-function research, the lockdowns, the vaccines, the censorship, and his own actions — all left unanswered.

It became clear: this was not an incidental refusal.  
It was a pre-planned strategy of total silence.

**Yesterday: the contempt vote**

On August 6, the Senate Homeland Security Committee voted 8-7 to hold Fauci in **contempt of Congress**.

Contempt of Congress means the legislature formally judges that someone is obstructing its investigative authority by refusing to answer questions or produce documents.  
It is a serious charge. The matter can now be referred to the Department of Justice, which may decide to pursue criminal prosecution.

Rand Paul intends to do exactly that.

**Josh Hawley’s trap**

During the hearing, Senator Josh Hawley deliberately asked the most innocent questions imaginable:

- “What day of the week is it today?”
- “What color tie are you wearing?”
- “What color is the carpet in front of you?”

Fauci gave the same answer every time:  
*“On the advice of counsel, I respectfully decline to answer based upon my rights under the Fifth Amendment…”*

Hawley then explained why he asked those questions.  
This was not a game.  
It was a test of whether Fauci was willing to answer *anything* at all.  
When someone refuses to state the color of his own tie, it shows he had decided in advance to answer nothing — not because he feared self-incrimination, but because he wanted to avoid any form of accountability.

**What is at stake**

This is not about a tie.  
This is about the man who for years was the highest medical authority in the United States.  
The man whose advice and decisions directly affected millions of lives, businesses, children, and entire societies.  
The man who claimed to be “the science.”

And he was not alone.  
In the Netherlands there was Marion Koopmans (@MarionKoopmans).  
In Belgium, Marc Van Ranst (@vanranstmarc).  
In almost every Western country, similar figures followed the same script, defended the same measures, and dismissed the same criticism as “disinformation.”

**#NameYourFauci**

When the central figure of this network refuses to answer even the most banal question, it says more about the scale of what may need to remain hidden than a hundred legal analyses ever could.

**OIM way out**

Silence is also an answer.  
And in this case, the silence is deafening.

**Question to you**

What does it say about the seriousness of the matter when someone who for years claimed to be “the science” — and whose local counterparts did the same everywhere — refuses to say what color his tie is?
