  ### RVN: Trump cuts off federal funding for transgender treatments on children
  
  This week the Trump administration made a definitive decision:  
  Medicaid and CHIP will no longer cover puberty blockers, cross-sex hormones, or gender surgeries for minors starting in October.
  
  No more federal tax money for these treatments on children under 18.
  
  **The narrative**
  
  This is immediately framed as:
  - An attack on trans children
  - Denying healthcare
  - Hate and ideology
  - Putting children in danger
  
  **The reality**
  
  This is not a ban. States can still pay for it themselves if they choose.  
  It is the end of federal funding for treatments that carry serious, often irreversible harm.
  
  What actually happens in these pathways:
  
  - **Puberty blockers** followed by cross-sex hormones in boys frequently result in an underdeveloped penis (micropenis) and almost always cause infertility.
  - Girls given testosterone face irreversible voice deepening, clitoral enlargement, infertility, and later severe vaginal atrophy.
  - Surgeries (mastectomy, genital surgery) performed on adolescents are permanent. There is no going back.
  - Bone density, sexual function, brain development, and long-term mental health outcomes show concerning results in multiple studies.
  
  More and more European countries (Sweden, Finland, Norway, England after the Cass Review) have already sharply restricted or stopped these treatments for minors — precisely because of the risks and the weak evidence base.
  
  The administration is essentially saying:  
  we will no longer use taxpayer money to fund experimental, irreversible interventions on children.
  
  **OIM way out**
  
  When it comes to minors, the bar for medical interventions should be especially high — particularly when those interventions cause infertility, sexual dysfunction, and permanent physical changes.
  
  Keep looking at the data and the long-term consequences.  
  Not at the emotional framing.
  
  **Question for you**
  
  Is protecting children from irreversible medical harm “hate”…  
  or exactly what a society is supposed to do?

### RVN: Trump cuts off federal funding for transgender treatments on children

This week the Trump administration made a definitive decision:  
Medicaid and CHIP will no longer cover puberty blockers, cross-sex hormones, or gender surgeries for minors starting in October.

No more federal tax money for these treatments on children under 18.

**The narrative**

This is immediately framed as:
- An attack on trans children
- Denying healthcare
- Hate and ideology
- Putting children in danger

**The reality**

This is not a ban. States can still pay for it themselves if they choose.  
It is the end of federal funding for treatments that carry serious, often irreversible harm.

What actually happens in these pathways:

- **Puberty blockers** followed by cross-sex hormones in boys frequently result in an underdeveloped penis (micropenis) and almost always cause infertility.
- Girls given testosterone face irreversible voice deepening, clitoral enlargement, infertility, and later severe vaginal atrophy.
- Surgeries (mastectomy, genital surgery) performed on adolescents are permanent. There is no going back.
- Bone density, sexual function, brain development, and long-term mental health outcomes show concerning results in multiple studies.

More and more European countries (Sweden, Finland, Norway, England after the Cass Review) have already sharply restricted or stopped these treatments for minors — precisely because of the risks and the weak evidence base.

The administration is essentially saying:  
we will no longer use taxpayer money to fund experimental, irreversible interventions on children.

**OIM way out**

When it comes to minors, the bar for medical interventions should be especially high — particularly when those interventions cause infertility, sexual dysfunction, and permanent physical changes.

Keep looking at the data and the long-term consequences.  
Not at the emotional framing.

**Question for you**

Is protecting children from irreversible medical harm “hate”…  
or exactly what a society is supposed to do?
