  ### RVN: Greta Thunberg – from Fridays for Future to Palestine
  
  In 2018 Greta Thunberg (then 15) started school strikes for the climate. That grew into Fridays for Future. In 2019 she gave her well-known speech at the UN climate summit in New York: “You have stolen my dreams and my childhood with your empty words.” The message was clear: the future of her generation was being taken away.[](<https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=o3FAE4e7Pok>)
  
  In the years that followed her focus shifted. After the Hamas attack of 7 October 2023 and the subsequent war in Gaza she became more active in pro-Palestinian protests. She took part in Gaza flotillas, regularly wore a keffiyeh and was detained several times. The German branch of Fridays for Future distanced itself from her.[](<https://www.ynetnews.com/article/bj2xp3ecgl>)
  
  This year she was again present at a UN climate summit (COP31 in Antalya). There she linked climate once more to other themes, including Gaza, and called for an oil embargo against Israel. At the same time a fragment from a German documentary circulated. In it she is told that a Fridays for Future demonstration is taking place in Berlin. She reacts with disbelief and laughter: “Wirklich? Die machen das immer noch?”[](<https://www.welt.de/vermischtes/article6af5803cd2f851d14ad004b6/greta-thunberg-lacht-ueber-deutsche-klima-proteste-die-machen-das-immer-noch.html>)
  
  Later she gave context on Instagram: that day she was involved in a blockade of a weapons manufacturer (Rheinmetall). The climate demo was, according to her, a police-approved action in a “bekendermaßen weißen Mittelschichtsgegend”, organised by the German climate movement that had earlier distanced itself from her because she had said Palestinians are “also people”.[](<https://www.bz-berlin.de/berlin/greta-thunberg-darum-lachte-sie-ueber-fridays-for-future-6af85a016df4bbeedf7a2f05>)
  
  ### What is actually on the record
  
  The 2019 speech is on video and widely distributed.  
  The shift to pro-Palestinian activism after 2023 is documented (protests, flotillas, detentions).  
  The laughter in the German documentary is recent and confirmed by multiple media outlets.  
  Thunberg herself later explained the context of that laughter.
  
  ### The pattern
  
  An activist who in 2019 put the future of “the children” at the centre later focuses on other conflict zones and reacts ironically to the continuation of the movement she helped start.  
  Media attention follows the new focus. The old focus (school strikes, “they take away my future”) fades into the background.
  
  Whether this is “acting” is not a fact. It is an interpretation. What is established is the chronology of the shift and the public reaction to the old movement.
  
  ### What this means
  
  The 2019 speech is still quoted as a moral anchor.  
  The recent statements and the laughter show that priorities have changed.  
  Both can exist side by side. The tension lies in the question whether the original urgency still carries the same weight, or whether attention has moved with another conflict.
  
  ### OIM way out
  
  Do not treat one framing (“she has always been sincere”) as the end point.  
  Do not treat the other framing (“she is an actor”) as the end point.
  
  Instead:  
  the video of the 2019 speech,  
  the documentation of her activities after 2023,  
  the fragment from the German documentary,  
  and her own later explanation of it.
  
  Primary records and the order in which they became available above a story that shows only the laughter or only the old speech.
  
  **Question for you**
  
  How often have you seen a public figure shift from one urgent theme to another…  
  without the original urgency being tested again?
