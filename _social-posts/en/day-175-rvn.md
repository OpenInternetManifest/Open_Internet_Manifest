### RVN: The photograph with Damascus

New York, UN week.  
Jetten and Máxima next to Ahmed al-Sharaa. President of Syria. Handshake. SANA puts it online. De Telegraaf calls him the butcher.

Two pictures.  
One: a reset. Assad is gone. Reconstruction. Europe talks again.  
The other: the same man, different jacket.

### Who he was

Abu Mohammad al-Jolani.  
Iraq, then Syria. In 2012 he founds Jabhat al-Nusra, al-Qaeda’s Syrian branch. Later he breaks with Baghdadi’s ISIS — not because the caliphate was too soft. Then Hayat Tahrir al-Sham. Idlib as an emirate: sharia, a secret service, a US bounty (millions on his head).

HTS ran a province as a state. No election. Taxes, prisons, sermons. The green flag that still sat on terror lists here.

December 2024: Assad flees. HTS takes Damascus. Jolani becomes al-Sharaa. Suit. Palace. This week Costa, von der Leyen, Jetten.

The rebrand is the policy.  
Not a footnote.

### What the photo does

A prime minister negotiates.  
A queen is the face of the Kingdom.

Together in one frame with a man whose former organisation still stood on the list here is not tea. It is a sentence: we do business again.

Whether that is wise is politics.  
Whether it is contested is the CV.

### The pattern

First the jihad.  
Then the emirate.  
Then the suit.  
Then the reception.

Anyone who only says “diplomacy” wipes Nusra.  
Anyone who only says “jihadist” pretends Syria has no government left.

The till is the CV next to the photo.  
Not the smile.

### OIM way out

Do not tear down Máxima.  
Do not make Jetten a traitor.

Do: which agreements. Return of Syrians. Sanctions. Who makes the law in Damascus — a parliament or the old chain.

The photo is the start of that table.  
Not the end.

**Question for you**

Do you look at the suit…  
or at the name that was on the list before?
