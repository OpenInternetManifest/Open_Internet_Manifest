### RVN: White House – the plug and the badge

Friday: Trump bars CNN, Politico and MS NOW from the White House. Fake news. Others may follow.  
Saturday: badges taken at the gate. CNN: illegal. Lawsuits incoming.

Two frames.  
One: the government chooses who may look. That is not a review. That is the plug.  
The other: the White House is not a right to a chair. Anyone who runs fiction need not sit in the front row.

Both can hold a piece.  
Neither is the file.

### What stands

Physical access is shut.  
The reporting continues, from the pavement.  
Trump gave no examples in the post. Cumulative irritation over two years, he said later. A list of rebutted items and a deadline was not there. A door was.  
“Other news outlets to follow.”

Earlier already: AP out of the pool over the Gulf of America. Same conflict, different shirt.  
Whoever may put the name on the map may also take the badge.

### The pattern

First the lie.  
Then the door.  
Then “press freedom” or “at last.”

The label does the work.  
The question who still gets in — and under which rule — stays in the annex.

Same reflex as ON out of the public system.  
A public building, selective access, a moral ending.

### OIM way out

Do not canonise CNN.  
Do not make the president editor-in-chief.

Do: which rule was on paper before the Truth Social line.  
Does it weigh as heavy for Fox as for CNN.  
What remains of a pool when the host picks the guests.

Press freedom is publishing.  
A badge is a chair.  
Pull those apart.

**Question for you**

When the door shuts…  
do you look first at the broadcast, or first at who holds the key?
