  ### RVN: In the mouth – when the prime minister says it
  
  Sunday, D66 congress. Jetten on groups that attack LGBT rights and minorities:  
  we will not let them win. We will smash them in the mouth.
  
  Monday: regret. Nicer language. Not literal. Passionate. Slip of the tongue.
  
  Everyone understood the metaphor.  
  That does not make the sentence innocent. It makes the apology thin.
  
  ### The test
  
  Say the same line as an MP for PVV or FVD.  
  The world is too small.
  
  Say it as prime minister, on the file D66 cares about most, and it becomes diction.
  
  Baudet took a bottle to the head. A weighted umbrella.  
  Then it was: stop whining. Political risk. He asked for it.
  
  Two weights.  
  One skull.
  
  ### What it is not
  
  No knife from Jetten.  
  It is a prime minister pointing at “those people” and choosing a mouth — in a country where the metaphor has already been carried out.
  
  Sunday he was taken off stage for Gaza protesters. Respect for their right.  
  Then the mouth. Two registers, one afternoon.
  
  ### The pattern
  
  First the camp.  
  Then the violence-word.  
  Then “not literally”.
  
  The standard holds until he uses it.  
  Then it is passion.
  
  ### OIM way out
  
  Do not make Jetten a brawler.  
  Do not make Baudet a saint.
  
  Do: would this line from Wilders lead the news.  
  Do: which group the prime minister may name this way, and who had to stop whining when the bottle fell.
  
  **Question for you**
  
  When the prime minister says “in the mouth”…  
  do you hear a slip, or a standard that only runs one way?

### RVN: In the mouth – when the prime minister says it

Sunday, D66 congress. Jetten on groups that attack LGBT rights and minorities:  
we will not let them win. We will smash them in the mouth.

Monday: regret. Nicer language. Not literal. Passionate. Slip of the tongue.

Everyone understood the metaphor.  
That does not make the sentence innocent. It makes the apology thin.

### The test

Say the same line as an MP for PVV or FVD.  
The world is too small.

Say it as prime minister, on the file D66 cares about most, and it becomes diction.

Baudet took a bottle to the head. A weighted umbrella.  
Then it was: stop whining. Political risk. He asked for it.

Two weights.  
One skull.

### What it is not

No knife from Jetten.  
It is a prime minister pointing at “those people” and choosing a mouth — in a country where the metaphor has already been carried out.

Sunday he was taken off stage for Gaza protesters. Respect for their right.  
Then the mouth. Two registers, one afternoon.

### The pattern

First the camp.  
Then the violence-word.  
Then “not literally”.

The standard holds until he uses it.  
Then it is passion.

### OIM way out

Do not make Jetten a brawler.  
Do not make Baudet a saint.

Do: would this line from Wilders lead the news.  
Do: which group the prime minister may name this way, and who had to stop whining when the bottle fell.

**Question for you**

When the prime minister says “in the mouth”…  
do you hear a slip, or a standard that only runs one way?
