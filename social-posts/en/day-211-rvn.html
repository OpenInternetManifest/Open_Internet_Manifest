RVN: Mosab Hassan Yousef – the Green Prince and the street

In November 2026 Mosab Hassan Yousef is giving interviews in the Netherlands, Switzerland and Italy. He is the son of Sheikh Hassan Yousef, one of the founders of Hamas. Between 1997 and 2007 he was an informant for Israel’s Shin Bet under the codename Green Prince. He wrote the book Son of Hamas about it. Later he became a Christian, moved to the US and is naturalised there. His family has disowned him. He lives under protection.

On one side: people who call him a traitor or distrust his story. On the other side: someone who comes from the inside and now says out loud what he sees in European streets.

What he has recently said

In interviews with among others WNL Op Zondag, the NZZ and Italian media:

He calls pro-Palestinian demonstrations in Europe “an invitation for chaos in their own country”. People who march have, according to him, no idea what they are supporting.

Hamas, according to him, is not a political movement with borders, but a religious one whose goal is the destruction of Israel and then Islamic rule further afield – he explicitly mentions Europe and “Rome”.

The two-state solution, according to him, died on 7 October 2023.

Israel is, according to him, doing the Palestinians a service by fighting Hamas, because Hamas uses its own people as a shield.

Iran is the main state sponsor of Hamas. That is no longer a theory: US Treasury estimates, sanctioned networks and documents found in Gaza (including transfers of tens of millions and a special budget line in the run-up to 7 October) have established it for years. Yousef calls Iran “the real master” of the organisation.

What is established and what is not

Established: he is the son of a Hamas founder, worked for years as a Shin Bet informant, wrote a book about it, and is now saying these things in public interviews. The financial and military support from Iran to Hamas is documented. He is on Hamas’s kill list, has been disowned by his family and lives under protection – that is publicly known and follows from his past as an informant.

Not established: whether every formulation in the media about his exact role inside Hamas is as precise as his own biography. Some pieces point out that the framing “son who was himself in Hamas” is sometimes presented more smoothly than the facts justify. He was the son and the informant.

What he says about the demonstrations and the goals of Hamas is his own account, based on what he saw from the inside. Whether that is the full truth, or the only truth, is another question. It is in any case not an anonymous internet theory.

OIM way out

Do not adopt one framing (“hero who speaks the truth”) or the other (“traitor and propagandist”).

Instead: read his own words in the interviews. Read his book. Look at what is happening in European streets and what the people there say they are supporting. Check the primary sources, including the documents on Iranian funding.

Verifiable statements from someone with a checkable history above a street narrative that makes no distinction between resistance and the ideology behind it.

Question for you

How often have you heard someone who comes from the inside…
and then only seen him as “the wrong side”?
